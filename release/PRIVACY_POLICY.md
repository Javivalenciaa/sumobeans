# Flag Fury - Privacy Policy

**Who we are.** Flag Fury is an independent browser game published on CrazyGames.

**What is stored.** Your progress (level, coins, cosmetics, settings and language) is stored through the CrazyGames SDK: in your CrazyGames account if you are logged in, or in your browser otherwise. We do not receive or read it.

**Online play.** To connect players, the game sends a nickname (your CrazyGames username if you are logged in, otherwise a random name such as "Player123"), your position, controls and chosen weapon and cosmetics to our relay server while a match is running. We do not store these messages and there is no chat. Standard server and network logs (for example IP address and time) may be kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting.

**Advertising.** Ads are served by CrazyGames and their privacy policy applies to any ad data. We do not use our own cookies, trackers or analytics.

**Children.** The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.

**Your choices.** You can clear your browser data, or contact CrazyGames, to remove stored progress. For any privacy question, contact us through the Flag Fury page on CrazyGames.

*(The same text is available inside the game: Settings > Privacy Policy, in English and Spanish.)*
