# Flag Fury - CrazyGames submission text

**Title:** Flag Fury

**Short description (1 line):** 3v3 first-person Capture the Flag with swords, spears and crossbows.

**Description:**
Flag Fury is a fast 3v3 capture-the-flag arena. Pick a sword, spear or crossbow, steal the enemy flag and bring it home before time runs out.
Matches last about 4 minutes on 4 very different maps: Dawn Bridges, Toy Factory (conveyor belts), Sky Canyon (jump pads) and Frozen Lake (slippery ice).
Play a local match against bots, jump into public multiplayer, or create a private room and invite friends with a code or link (bots optional).
Level up, earn coins, complete daily missions, climb the ranked leagues and unlock hats, backpacks and trails.

**How to play (desktop):** WASD move - Mouse look - Click attack - Shift or right click dash - Space jump - E use item - Tab pause.
**How to play (mobile):** left stick to move, drag the right side to look, buttons to attack, jump and dash.

**Tags:** capture the flag, multiplayer, 3d, first person, fighting, arena, action, bots, ctf, team

**Category:** Action / Multiplayer

**Languages:** English (default), Spanish

**Devices:** Desktop and mobile (touch controls included)

**Multiplayer:** ON - 3v3 (6 players per room), lobby size 6. Private rooms and invite links supported.
